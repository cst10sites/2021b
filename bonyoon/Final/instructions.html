Steps Instructions
Step 1 Cut your vegetables into pieces that fit into your buns
Step 2 Butter all of your buns on the side that the patty will go on
Step 3 Mix some mayonaise with either dijon mustard, yellow mustart, ketchup, and barbecue sause in 4 seperate bowls
Step 4 Preheat your grill to medium
Step 5 Divide your ground beef into 4 equal pieces that are 1 inch wider than your burger bun.
Step 6 season your pattys with salt and pepper
Step 7 Grill your pattys for 3 to 5 mins on each side
Step 8 Toast your buns until golden brown
Step 9 Combine your patty, buns, sauce and vegetable the way you want
