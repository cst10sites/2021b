How to make a grilled cheese sandwhich

Introduction

Today i will be teaching you how to make a grilled cheese sandwhich. This is one of my favorite foods because it is easy to make and it tastes good.

Ingredients

  • two pieces of bread
  • mayonaise (10 ½ g)
  • Your favorite type of cheese (1 ½ ounces)

Instructions

  1. spread 5 ¼ g of mayonaise on one side of each piece of toast
  2. Put your cheese in between your bread with the buttered sides facing out
  3. Place the sandwich on a medium high pan until golden brown on both sides
