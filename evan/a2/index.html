Grilled cheese recipe

In this tutorial I will be teaching you how to make a grilled cheese sandwich.

Ingredients and Equipment

  • The type of bread you like
  • Any type of cheese
  • Butter
  • A pan
  • A stovetop
  • A spatula

Recipe

  1. Spread ⅛ cup of butter on the outside of each peice of bread and put the cheese on the bread
  2. Put your bread with cheese inside a pan that is a low to medium heat(280°F)
  3. Put pressure on the bread to help melt the cheese
  4. Flip it and put pressure wait another 2-3 minutes than take it out of the pan
  5. Cut it to your preference and enjoy!!!!
