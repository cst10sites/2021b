Making Toad in The hole

Hello, and welcome to my epic kitchen where we make epic foods with the epic chef. Today we will be making toad in the hole a British recipe Where you place the egg inside the toast. Listed below are the instructions And the list of ingredients also, here's a picture to warm your memory a bit

Click the picture.


Ingredients

  • 1 Egg
  • ¼ of Butter
  • 1 Slice of bread

Instructions

  1. Cut a 3-in. hole in the middle of the bread and discard. In a small skillet, melt the butter; place the bread in the skillet.
  2. Place egg in the hole. Cook for about 2 minutes over medium (330°f) heat until the bread is lightly browned. Turn and cook the other side until egg yolk is almost set. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Take out your utensils and dig in.
