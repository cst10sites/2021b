How to make Kraft Dinner

Today I am going to show you how to make Kraft Dinner. This recipe is very easy and can be done by anyone. Make sure you are comfortable using a stove as you need to use it for this recipe.

Ingredients and Equipment

  • One pot
  • Stove
  • 1-1½ boxes of Kraft Dinner

Instructions

  1. Add water and boil to 100°C. Add noodles to pot and cook until noodles are ready
  2. Add butter (optional) and cheese powder to noodles and stir
  3. When all noodles are coated, add ½ cups of milk (optional)
  4. Serve while still hot
