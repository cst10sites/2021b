Civilization VI Guide - Core Gameplay

Yields

Yields are produced by your cities.

  • Food is used to grow a city's population. Each city has its own food, so be sure to build food-producing buildings in every city.
  • Production is similar to Food in that it is individually tracked for each city. Production is used to build buildings and train units. The more production a city has, the faster it builds and trains.
  • Science is used to research Technologies in the Technology tree, which unlock new buildings and upgrades. The more Science you have, the faster Technologies are researched.
  • Culture is almost identical to Science, except that it is insted used to research Civics from the Civic tree, which unlock dimplomatic and political actions.
  • Gold accumulates over time and is shared between all of your cities. Gold can be used to instantly build a building or instantly train a unit.
  • Faith, much like Gold, accumulates over time and can be spent to purchase missionaries.

Resources

There are 3 types of resources in the game, each when improved with the correct improvement (farm, mine, fishery etc), give bonuses

  • Bonus (yellow) resources (such as wheat, fish, and stone) give extra Food, Production, or Gold when improved.
  • Luxury (purple) resources (such as spice, silk, and furs) give Amenities (which boost population growth and efficiency). However, producing multiple copies of the same Luxury will not give Amenities, so be sure to trade them with other civilizations.
  • Strategic (red) resources (such as iron, coal, and oiil) are used to produce more powerful units later in the game.

Combat and Unit Types

There are several different unit types in Civilization, each with their own abilities.

Land Unit Types

  • Melee units attack adjacent units.
  • Ranged units can attack units from 2 tiles away.
  • Light Calvary are fast, but take more damage.
  • Heavy Calvary are slower than Light Calvary, but have more health.
  • Anti-Calvary units deal bonus damage to Light and Heavy Calvary.
  • Siege units are ideal for attacking and sieging cities.
  • Recon units are for scouting and exploring territory
  • Support units do a variety of things to support other units.

Naval Unit Types

  • Naval Melee units attack adjacent units.
  • Naval Ranged units can attack units from 2 tiles away.
  • Naval Raider units can only be seen by other Naval Raiders.

Air Unit Types

  • Fighters are the main type of air unit, and are effective against almost all land units.
  • Bombers deal extra damage to cities. Additionally, they can detonate nuclear bombs.
