Civilization VI Guide - Getting Started

The Early Game

A game of Civilization starts with the player picking a historical leader to play as. The base game includes 19 leaders, however, with all expansion content installed there are a total of 54 leaders. Each leader leads a civilizaton (such as Rome, Britain, or Japan) which has multiple bonuses and abilities.

Once you have chosen your leader, you will be presented with a map of hexagonal tiles, on which you have two units: a Settler and a Warrior.

  • The Settler is used to found your cities - try settling your first (capitol) city next to a river or coast for a boost to poulation growth.
  • The Warrior is used for combat, but for now use it to explore more of the map.

Your city can produce a variety of units and buildings.

  • Scouts move faster than warriors, but have little combat strength. They are ideal for exploration
  • Slingers can attack enemies from 2 tiles away (instead of only being able to attack adjacent enemies).
  • Builders can build improvements (farms, mines, etc) on tiles with resources.

While exploring the map, you may encounter barbarians. Use your Warriors and Slingers to defeat them, and send a unit to their camp to raid it for a reward.

Winning the Game

There are 4 ways to win a game of Civilization:

  • Domination Victory requires you to conquer the capital city of every civilization
  • Science Victory requires you to complete the three Space Race missions, which are available in the late game. A large amount of Production is necessary for this.
  • Cultural Victory requires you to become the world's dominant culture. This is done primarily by building World Wonders.
  • Religion Victory requires your religion to be the dominant world religion. This is done by using your Faith to purchase missionaries, and sending those missionaries to foreign cities.
