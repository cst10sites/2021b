Chocolate Chip Cookie Recipe

Introduction

Chocolate Chip cookies. A classic baked treat to have and personally one of my favourites. Here I will teach you how to make some from a recipe I found online. If you desire you can add chopped nuts, but it isn't necessary to complete the recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 1⁄4 cups of all purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon of baking soda
  • 1⁄2 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 cup of softened butteer
  • 3⁄4 of a cup of granulated sugar
  • 3⁄4 of a cup of packed brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla
  • 2 cups of semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup of coarsely chopped nuts(optional)

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 365°F then mix together your flour, baking soda and salt in a small bowl.
  2. Then in another larger bowl beat together the butter and both of the sugers either with an electric mixer on medium speed, or a spoon for one minute until your mixture is fluffy. Make sure when you are doig this that you scrape the side of the bowl occasionally.
  3. In the same bowl beat in the egg and vanilla until it is encorperated. Then take the small bowl with the flour mixture and put the contents in there with the contents in the larger bowl. Stir until it is just blended (the dough will be stiff). Stir in the chocolate chips and nuts.
  4. Drop rounded tablespoonfuls of cookie dough on a cookie sheet 2 inches apart.
  5. Bake for 8-10 minutes (the cookies should be light brown). After they have baked let cool for 2 minutes then transfer to a cooling rack to let cool fully for 30 minutes.

Storing the Cookies

Put in chocolate chip cookies in an airtight container and make sure you eat them all within one week so that they don't go stale :)
