How Climate Change has Impacted Polar Bears

As you already know polar bears live in the arctic. Since the arctic is mainly comprised of sea ice their main risk as an effect of global warming is the loss of sea ice from the temperatures in the arctic are rising. Without as much ice it makes polar bears have more difficulty hunting for seals. Another difficulty these bears face as a result of rising temperature's is their den's collapsing. Female polar bears build dens for their young for when they give birth. Without them is can lead to a higher risk of disease as well as contamination.

Decline of Polar Bears

By 2050 the polar bear population has an estimated decline of one third. With the temperatures increasing rapidly all the things that I have previously metioned that are impacting the polar bear population are just going to get worse and worse is we don't take any action for it.
