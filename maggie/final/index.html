Polar Bear

About

Weighing in at 900lbs - 1600lbs ursus maritimus or otherwise known as the polar bear is the majestic white bear that lives in the arctic.

Diet and Habitat

Polar bears are carnivorous mammals that live in various places around the Arctic. To keep warm they have a thick layer of fur and below that they have body fat to keep them warm.

Seals are what their diet consits most out of, but they occasionally eat whale carcasess and bird eggs. A polar bear catches a seal by waiting for the seal to come up for air to breathe. Then the bear grabs or bites the seal and brings it up to the surface. Polar bears spend the majority of their time in the day hunting for food. Worrying about predetors isn't much of a problem for them (except for native hunters) as they are at the top of the food chain.

Behaviors

Polar bears are solitary animals for the most part, the only time they are not is in mating season. When a mother has her cubs she is vedry protective towards them. These bears are pretty smart creatures as well as playful ones. As a sign a polar bear will want to play with another it will move it's head side to side.
