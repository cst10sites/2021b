Introductions

This easy to make protein filled breakfast, this super easy recipe is some delicious bacon, some whole wheat bread and the main part the scrambled eggs. This fast and easy recipe only takes 30 minutes to make, so its amazing for weekend mornings. And plus this recipes will only cost you around 20$.

Ingredients

  • 2 pieces pf bacon
  • 50g of cheese
  • 10ml of milk
  • A pinch ofsalt
  • A bit of grounded black Pepper
  • 15g of butter(or~15ml of melted butter)
  • 4 medium eggs

Steps

  1. Heat pan to 260°c or on high
  2. Grill the bacon utill its crispy
  3. Put the eggs in a small bowl, beat lightly, then put them in the sauce pan
  4. add the butter and milk
  5. put the pan on low heat or 93°c and slowly stir, till the eggs thicken
  6. then add the cheese
  7. while the eggs are cooking put some bread to toast on the side.
  8. Arrange the bread, Bacon and eggs in order
  9. Serve warm
