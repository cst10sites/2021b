Chocolate Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1¼ cups margarine, softened
  • 2 cups white sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ⅛ teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup chopped walnuts
  1. Preheat oven to 175oC
  2. In a bowl, mix the margarine and sugar until smooth.Add eggs one at a time, then the vanilla. Add the flour, cocoa, baking soda and salt into the mixture intil blended. Mix in the walnuts and place mixture in spoonfuls onto ungreased cookie sheet.
  3. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes in oven. Cool after taking out on wire racks.
